MIDI device

Experimental. The keys sound with a piano (SOUND turns it off, for keyboards with their own speakers). In SCALES the keys light the notes you play, PRACTICE listens to them instead of the microphone, and a chord you hold is named and shown on your instrument. ASSIGN ties pads, buttons and knobs to the scale and JAM buttons, the JAM effects, the metronome and the tempo. Chrome, Edge or Firefox; not Safari.

Audio input

--

          

          

            Plug a guitar or any instrument into a USB audio interface (or the phone's adapter) and pick it here. The tuner and the looper
            then listen to it directly, with no echo cancelling, noise reduction or gain control, instead of the microphone. TEST shows the level, the note
            and what the browser really gave: channels, sample rate and latency.
