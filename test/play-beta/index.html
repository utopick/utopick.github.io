Audio input and output

GAIN0 dB
Use headphones or an interface: with the phone's own mic and speaker the sound feeds back. Chrome sends the sound straight to the output you choose. Firefox can only do it through its media player, about 50 ms later: in Firefox leave it on system default and pick the output in the system sound settings.

Engine

The defaults are the fastest found in our tests. If the sound crackles, try a bigger buffer. Changing the rate, the buffer or the raw input restarts listening.

MIDI controller

A keyboard plays the synth. Knobs and pads follow the screen: on the rig the knobs of the unit (pad 1 turns it on or off, pad 2 is TEST), in the mixer the volumes (the master first), on the synth its knobs, on the pedal board, looper, tracks and voice the pads in order from the top left. MAP teaches the app your device, ASSIGN ties a control to a function (hold a pad for three seconds). Chrome, Edge or Firefox, not Safari.
