Add songs or backing tracks from this device (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A...). They are kept here, never uploaded. Tap one to play it, again to stop. They go to the TRACKS fader in the MIXER, not through the board.
Each source has its volume and a mute (M); the master sets what reaches your headphones. The bars move while sound plays. The speaker at the end of the board opens this tab.

Board

EXPORT keeps the order of the board and the knobs and switches of every pedal, amp and cab in a small .json file, to share or keep; IMPORT loads one. Your board is also kept on this device by itself.

Theme

Stats

Numbers from the browser while listening. The output figures are the browser's own estimate; LATENCY TEST measures the whole trip (sound out, your pick, sound in).

Audio input and output

GAIN0 dB
Use headphones or an interface: with the phone's own mic and speaker the sound feeds back. Chrome sends the sound straight to the output you choose. Firefox can only do it through its media player, about 50 ms later: in Firefox leave it on system default and pick the output in the system sound settings.

Engine

The defaults are the fastest found in our tests. If the sound crackles, try a bigger buffer. Changing the rate, the buffer or the raw input restarts listening.
